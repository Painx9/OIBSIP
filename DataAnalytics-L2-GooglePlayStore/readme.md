# Google Play Store App Analysis

## Overview
An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project focusing on the Android app market to uncover trends in app categories, user ratings, and monetization strategies. 

## Key Insights
* **Market Saturation vs. Demand:** The `Family` and `Game` categories contain the highest volume of apps available. However, `Game` and `Communication` apps drive the vast majority of total user installs.
* **Rating Distributions:** User ratings are heavily left-skewed, peaking around 4.3 to 4.5. 
* **Pricing Dynamics:** Paid apps generally maintain slightly higher median ratings with fewer low-rating outliers compared to free apps, likely due to the absence of ads and higher development standards.

## Tech Stack
* **Python:** Pandas, NumPy
* **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
